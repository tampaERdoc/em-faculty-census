# EM Faculty Census Explorer

A searchable web tool for a national census of emergency medicine faculty at all 304 ACGME-accredited EM residency programs: 12,911 faculty-program records, compiled in September 2026 (12,882 in the paper's analysis, which was frozen on September 24 with 12,905 records before 23 listings were removed on October 2–3: fifteen that are not faculty, one administrative-support listing and fourteen fellows or students listed with the faculty; one listing of a physician who died in 2023; and seven duplicate listings of the same person at the same institution; plus 29 fellowship directors added on September 26 from the SAEM Fellowship Directory, marked on their records).

**Live site:** https://tampaerdoc.github.io/em-faculty-census/

## What you can do

- Search for a program or a person by name, institution, city, or state.
- Filter by normalized rank title (the rank analyzed in the paper), department chair (academic or hospital chairs listed by name, or programs with no chair identified), and leadership role (program director, vice chair, student clerkship director, and more); choosing a rank, chair, or role lists the matching people.
- Filter by the department/program described title, the title exactly as the department or program describes it before it is normalized to a rank (for example Clinical Assistant Professor, Assistant Clinical Professor, or Assistant Professor of Clinical Emergency Medicine). Type part of a title to find its variants, then tick them one by one or select all shown.
- Filter programs by AAU, Vizient, and Blue Ridge (any combination), marker phenotype, program type, program length (3-year or 4-year), DO origin, ACGME accreditation era, state, hospital ownership, and ED staffing. Program types (regrouped September 29, 2026): **research-intensive academic** (AAU member or NIH-ranked in the Blue Ridge table, 75 programs), **Vizient academic** (Vizient marker only, 39), **university-based academic** (no marker, 27), **corporate-affiliated** (76), **community-based** (82), and military (5); the three academic types together form the **academic group** (141 programs), selectable with one click and available as a summary breakdown.
- Filter the 76 **corporate-affiliated** programs by **owner–employer designation**, the owner of the primary teaching hospital paired with the employer of its emergency physicians (for example *HCA–HCA* where HCA Healthcare employs them directly, *HCA–TeamHealth*, *HCA–Envision*, or *Non-profit–USACS*), by the ED physician employer itself (TeamHealth, Envision, US Acute Care Solutions, Vituity, ApolloMD, SCP Health, regional and independent groups, or the for-profit owner), and by the for-profit company that owns the hospital (HCA Healthcare, Tenet, UHS, and others). Employers were verified program by program on September 29, 2026 from dated sources and the CMS Medicare clinician file; each program shows its source, date, and confidence. Type *HCA-Envision programs*, *all TeamHealth programs*, or *HCA programs* in Search or Ask the data.
- Filter people further by degree and Scopus or Google Scholar h-index range.
- Summarize any selection: n, mean, median, and interquartile range of the Scopus (or Google Scholar) h-index, overall and by normalized rank title, department/program described title, leadership role, program, program type, academic group, program length, research stratum (research-intensive, Vizient only, or no marker), accreditation era, or program origin, with a box-plot figure. Download the figure as PNG or SVG and the summary as CSV.
- Tick the box beside any program or person to limit the summary to your selection: one program gives its faculty, two or more programs are compared side by side. Ticked rows stay selected across searches, and a copied link keeps them.
- Sort any column, including AAU, Vizient, and Blue Ridge.
- Open a program to see everything recorded for it, including all of its faculty.
- Filter people by **program director**: the residency program director designated in the census, or the director of any fellowship listed in the SAEM Fellowship Directory (entries dated 2024 or later, confirmed on institutional pages; a director not in the directory is marked on the department chair's designation, stated on the profile). The summary can be grouped the same way, and *Ask the data* understands questions such as *how many ultrasound fellowship directors are there* or *residency program directors versus fellowship directors*.
- Open a faculty record to see its public professional email address where one was found (5,984 records), with its source: a faculty or professional page, or the author contact in a published article or document, whose current mailbox is not verified. CSV exports include the address and its source.
- Export any search as a CSV, or copy a link that reopens the same search.
- **Ask the data**: type a plain-language question and get the numbers, a figure, a table, and a link to the same selection in the explorer. For example: *show me the h-index distribution of chairs versus assistant professors*, *compare USF rank distribution and h-index to HCA Brandon*, *median Scopus h-index by rank at academic vs corporate-affiliated programs*, *how many program directors have an h-index of at least 10*, *list 4-year programs in Florida*, *who is the chair at Johns Hopkins*. It is a rule-based reader, not an AI model: it recognizes the census vocabulary (normalized ranks, described titles, leadership roles, degrees, program types, research markers, program length, accreditation era or year, states, health systems, and program names, nicknames, or ACGME IDs) and the words *versus*, *compare*, *by*, *how many*, *share*, *list*, *top*, and *who is*. Every answer says how the question was read. Nothing typed is sent anywhere; the reading and the arithmetic happen in the browser.

Definitions are on the site under **About the data**.

## How it works

It's a static site with no server and no dependencies: `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`, and one data file, `census-data.json`. GitHub Pages serves it from the `main` branch.

## Updating the data

Replace `census-data.json` with a newly generated file and commit it. The site redeploys automatically.

## Validation audits (1–3 October 2026)

Fourteen audits of the extracted values were run after the paper's analytic freeze and applied to this dataset on 1–3 October 2026, all using an AI system (OpenAI ChatGPT GPT-6 Astra) that took part in the original collection, unblinded; independent human review is pending.

- **Audit 1.** A stratified random source recheck of 300 faculty-program records (four Scopus provenance bands × seven ranks × six program types, seed `EM-census-validation-2026-09-30-v1`): rank agreed in 265 of 273 resolvable comparisons (97%); previously matched Scopus profiles agreed in 131 of 138 (95%; six of the seven differences were one citation accrued since September); but a matching profile with h > 0 was found for 62 of 150 records that had been assigned 0.
- **Audit 2.** A targeted recheck of all 83 full-professor listings with a reported Scopus 0: 63 had a profile with h > 0, 6 displayed 0, 6 completed negative searches, 8 unresolved.
- **Audit 3.** A targeted recheck of the 329 associate-professor listings with a reported Scopus 0: 66 confirmed discrepancies (identity confidence high for 58, moderate for 8) were transferred and applied; the other retained zeros were not transferred and stay as recorded (the reported zeros left without an audit result were searched in audit 14).
- **Audit 4.** A single authenticated author search for each of the 2,262 assistant-professor listings with an assigned Scopus 0: 1,496 searches returned candidate profiles (7,798 captured with affiliations, documents, h-indices and name variants) and 766 returned none. Candidates are search results, not confirmed identities, so only those meeting the dataset's original matching rule were applied (one name-matched candidate currently affiliated with the person's own institution, no competing candidate, no truncated result list): 38 matched it at the first pass and 36 were applied (28 with h > 0, 8 displaying 0); two were not (one match with h 1 because the reported 0 is the senior author's explicit adjudication; one because a second same-institution profile of the name was found). The other 1,460 listings with candidates await a publication-level identity check, the 766 searches that returned nothing are recorded as searched but not as completed negative searches (name variants were not searched), and 208 assigned zeros of 21 programs are absent from the search file (they were searched in audit 14).
- **Audit 5.** The 614 instructor listings with a reported Scopus 0 (first and last name; up to ten candidates captured; no search errors): 58 were already linked to a Scopus profile and were rechecked against it (50 reconfirmed at 0; 6 were not among the ten candidates returned and were not reopened; one now displays h 1 and was updated; one displays h 10 against an explicit adjudication of 0, which is retained and flagged for decision). The other 555 were screened with the same rule: 3 met it (2 with h > 0, 1 displaying 0), 351 with candidates await a publication-level identity check, and 201 returned no candidate (a 556th listing, with candidates held, was administrative support rather than faculty and was removed from the dataset on 2 October).
- **Audit 6.** The 1,787 listings with no academic rank and a reported Scopus 0: 169 were linked to a profile (138 reconfirmed at 0; 26 not reopened; 4 now display h 1 and were updated, on three physicians, one of whom is listed at two records; one displays h 9 against an explicit adjudication of 0, retained and flagged for decision). Of the other 1,618, 21 matched the rule: 17 were applied (12 with h > 0, 5 displaying 0) and four were not (two because the reported 0 rests on an explicit adjudication or a final identity review, two because the analyst review found a probable second profile of the same person); 981 listings with candidates, including those four, remain unresolved and 620 returned no candidate. Thirteen listings of this audit (eleven University of Florida fellows and two Baylor College of Medicine students listed with the faculty) were removed from the dataset on 2 October as not faculty: 10 with candidates held, 2 that returned no candidate, and one linked profile reconfirmed at 0.
- **Audit 7.** The last 403 listings with a reported Scopus 0 (367 assistant professors, 16 other titles, 9 associate professors, 7 emeritus, 4 full professors): 347 were linked to a profile (293 reconfirmed at 0; 48 not reopened; 5 now display h 1 and were updated; one displays h 1 where the register's 0 is a person-level recalculation across split profiles, unchanged). Of the other 56, none met the rule, 36 with candidates remain unresolved and 20 returned no candidate.
- **Audits 8-13.** A recheck of the Scopus author profile already linked to every full professor (1,344), associate professor (1,781), assistant professor (2,814), instructor (309), faculty member with no rank (1,056) and emeritus or other-title faculty member (121) with a reported h-index above 0, comparing the value the profile displays with the recorded one (the assistant-professor, instructor, no-rank and emeritus/other rechecks queried every stored author ID directly). The linked author ID is the identity key, so the check confirms the displayed value of that profile, not that the profile belongs to the person. 7,212 of 7,425 matched exactly (97.1%) and the other 213 differed (206 higher and 7 lower; every difference was one point except one decrease of two points): among full professors 1,274 exact, 68 higher and 2 lower; among associate professors 1,712 exact, 67 higher and 2 lower; among assistant professors 2,758 exact, 53 higher and 3 lower; among instructors 306 exact and 3 higher; among faculty with no rank 1,044 exact and 12 higher; among emeritus and other-title faculty 118 exact and 3 higher; none unresolved. 209 were updated to the displayed value in these audits and 3 more had already been updated; one kept the recorded value. Three cases needed the senior author's decision, taken on 2 October after the profiles and their publications were read: a full professor whose linked profile now shows 0 while a second profile of the same physician (confirmed on publications) shows 2 was updated to 2; an assistant professor whose linked profile now shows 4 against 6 recorded (a two-point decrease, outside the one-point rule) while a second profile of the same name shows 3 was updated to 4 (the two profiles together would give 6); and a faculty member with no rank whose linked profile shows 29 against 28 recorded but contains two documents by other authors kept 28, the value recalculated without them. The five audit 1 profiles that had displayed one point more than the recorded value were updated by the same rule. Possible second profiles with the same name at the same institution (245 candidates on 225 listings) were flagged on the full- and associate-professor records; the assistant-professor, instructor, no-rank and emeritus/other files carry no candidate list, so that screen could not be repeated for them. Nothing was combined. One of the 1,056 faculty members with no rank, a University of Florida fellow, was removed from the dataset on 2 October as not faculty.
- **Audit 14 (3 October).** A record-by-record authenticated Scopus author search of the 471 listings with a reported 0 that no earlier audit file covered (261 associate professors, 208 assistant professors and one emeritus listing, 424 of them assigned zeros, and one fellowship director added after the freeze): a full-name query and a first-initial query for each, up to ten candidates captured per query with affiliation, documents and h-index, and the existing author link reopened where there was one. The AI system returned 53 profiles with h > 0, 51 matched profiles displaying 0, 38 searches that returned no author and 329 listings it could not resolve, with no search left unperformed. Every proposed profile was then checked by the analysis team against the person's publications (PubMed) and institutional pages before anything was applied. Of the 53 with h > 0, 21 were confirmed as proposed, 1 was replaced by the correct profile (the proposed one was another person of the same name), 5 probable profiles that could not be established are kept at 0 unless the department or the person confirms them (senior author's decision, 3 October), and 26 were rejected: 25 belonged to another person (most often a researcher of the same or a similar name at another institution or in another field) and 1 was not supported by the evidence. Of the 51 at 0, 42 were the linked profile reconfirmed, 6 were confirmed new matches and 3 belonged to another person. The dataset's matching rule applied to the unresolved candidates found two more profiles (one with h 4, one displaying 0), both confirmed on publications. Applied: 23 listings now carry an observed h-index above 0 (13 associate and 10 assistant professors; h 1 to 17, median 3) and 7 an observed 0; the 38 searches that returned no author are recorded in the match status with the zero kept (name variants were not searched); 4 linked profiles that the search did not return were not reopened; and 356 assigned zeros with candidates (323 unresolved, 28 whose selected profile was rejected and 5 held) keep their value with a caution.
- **Rank re-audits R1 to R6 (2–3 October).** Separately from the h-index audits, the academic rank of every listing recorded as full professor (1,368), associate professor (2,058), assistant professor (5,622), instructor (920), no rank (2,827), or emeritus or another title (162) was re-examined against current institutional pages with the same AI system (unblinded), and each proposed change was read against the source by the analysis team before it was applied (a structured title field on a university profile or appointment record outranks free-text biography or a lagging department roster; a later dated page outranks an earlier one; pages that disagree are a conflict, not a change). In the first five rounds (R1 to R5, October 2), of the 12,790 distinct records (five are in both the full- and associate-professor files), 6,682 ranks were confirmed as recorded, 45 were changed (four to emeritus, six promotions to full professor, thirteen promotions to associate professor, six to assistant professor, two changes to no rank, the register's category for a listing without an academic rank, and fourteen University of Chicago listings with the title Clinical Associate recorded in the non-ladder category other, as the five University of Illinois Peoria clinical associates already were; ten of the promotions are University of Alabama at Birmingham promotions effective on 1 October 2026, after the 7–16 September collection window, and one change is on a fellowship-director record), fifteen listings that are not faculty were removed from the dataset (one administrative-support listing, which three university pages describe as such, twelve University of Florida fellows listed in the department's separate fellows section and two Baylor College of Medicine students), 35 with conflicting current sources keep the recorded rank with a flag, and 6,013 whose rank could not be independently re-read keep the recorded rank, neither confirmed nor contradicted. Fourteen proposed rank changes were not applied as proposed: five demotions from full professor (four contradicted by the title field of the official profile, one an unresolved conflict between two official pages), three assistant-professor changes (two to a non-rank category for the job title 'Specialist', which is not an academic rank, and one promotion where two official pages of the same university disagree), one instructor promotion where the department's own roster card still prints the instructor title, four assistant-professor proposals at one health system where the AI system had read the title line of the neighbouring roster card, and one full-professor proposal resting on a single sentence of biography; one more, a Chicago listing proposed as clinical associate, was applied as assistant professor because the university profile and the physician finder print that title. Five proposed removals of fellows in faculty-level roles (employed junior-faculty or administrative-leadership fellows) were not applied and are flagged for decision. The sixth round (R6, emeritus and other titles, 162 listings, 18 of them already re-examined in an earlier round) changed six University of Virginia listings that had been recorded as professor emeritus although each person's own profile shows an active rank (four associate and two assistant professors), refined eleven titles within the same category (seven Northwestern adjunct lecturers, one Stanford advanced lecturer, one Michigan clinical professor emerita and two University of New Mexico lecturers), removed one listing of a physician who died in 2023 (an in-memoriam entry on the program page), kept one emeritus listing with conflicting sources flagged, and did not apply one proposal whose cited page shows only a job title. Across the six rounds, 12,934 distinct listings (every listing the dataset has held) were re-examined: 6,797 ranks were confirmed as recorded, 51 were changed, 11 titles were refined, 16 listings were removed, 36 keep the recorded rank with a conflict flag and 6,023 could not be independently re-read. A separate scan for the same person listed twice at the same institution (same Scopus author profile) found seven duplicate listings (three at the University of Virginia and one each at Creighton–Phoenix, Henry Ford St. John, Old Dominion and Loma Linda, the last under a misspelled name); one listing of each pair was removed on 3 October.

Applied: 251 recovered values replaced assigned or observed zeros (sample 57, full professors 63, associate professors 66, assistant professors 28, instructors 2, No Rank 12, audit 14 23); 10 linked profiles recorded at 0 were updated to the h 1 each now displays (a citation accrued since the collection); 27 recovered profiles displaying 0 are now observed zeros; 22 completed negative searches (audits 1 and 2) are labelled as such in the value basis, and the 38 audit-14 searches that returned no author are recorded in the match status; 8 ranks were set to the current official source (date of change not established); 4 matched profiles found to contain another author's work carry a caution; unresolved cases keep their value with a caution. 214 linked positive profiles were updated to the value they now display, each by one point except one decrease of two points (audit 8: 69 higher and 1 lower; audit 9: 67 higher and 2 lower; audit 10: 50 higher and 3 lower; audit 11: 3 higher; audit 12: 11 higher; audit 13: 3 higher; the five audit 1 profiles: 5 higher). The update rule applies to an identity-verified linked profile whose displayed value differs from the recorded one (a profile recorded as 0 that now displays h ≥ 1 in audits 5–7; a positive profile that displays a value one point different in audits 8–13; a difference of two points, a possible second profile of the same name and a higher value on a profile already recorded as containing another author's publications were not applied automatically but decided case by case on 2 October, as described above); profiles that were not reopened keep the value read at the collection.

An assigned 0 means that no profile was found at collection, not that the person has no publications. 4,832 assigned zeros remain (of 5,120 at the freeze, thirteen of which were removed from the dataset on 2 October as not faculty): 3,174 searched with candidates returned but no identity resolved (2,818 in audits 4–7 and 356 in audit 14), 1,604 searched once with no candidate returned, 38 whose audit-14 searches on full name and first initial returned no author, 14 from the full-professor audit (6 completed negative searches, 8 unresolved), and 2 linked profiles that display a positive value against the explicit adjudication of 0. No assigned zero is now without an audit result. The recovered counts are floors on the false zeros, not estimates of them: in the random sample, a matching profile with h > 0 was found for 41% of the assigned zeros (design-weighted floor 28%). Positive values are more stable: the direct rechecks agree with the recorded value for 97.1% of 7,425 linked profiles (94.8% of full professors, 96.1% of associate professors, 98.0% of assistant professors, 99.0% of instructors, 98.9% of faculty with no rank, 97.5% of emeritus and other-title faculty), and every difference is one point except one decrease of two points. Every listing now shows its audit disposition on the faculty record and in the CSV column "Validation audit (October 1–3, 2026)".

## Corrections

Every value comes from a public source, but rosters and profiles change. To report an error, open the record on the site and choose **Report a correction**, or [open an issue](https://github.com/tampaERdoc/em-faculty-census/issues/new).
